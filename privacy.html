Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How we collect, safeguard, and respect your personal data when you visit our guild.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when creating an account, placing an order, or subscribing to our newsletter (e.g., name, shipping address, email).

Section 02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to fulfill orders, process secure payments, send tracking updates, and deliver weekly clone recipes if subscribed.

Section 03

Cookies & Session Tracking

We use essential session cookies to maintain your shopping cart contents and remember your dark/light mode and layout preferences.

Section 04

Data Security & Encryption

All customer data is encrypted using SSL protocols and stored on secure servers with strict access controls limiting internal access.

Section 05

Third-Party Service Providers

We share shipping addresses exclusively with trusted logistics partners (e.g., FedEx, UPS) and payment processors for order fulfillment.

Section 06

Your Data Rights & Opt-Out

You have the right to request access, correction, or complete deletion of your personal account data by contacting our support team.

Section 07

Children's Privacy Protection

Our store does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 21.

Section 08 & 09

Policy Changes & Consent

By using our platform, you consent to our privacy practices. Any updates to this policy will be posted directly on this page.

Section 10

Data Protection Officer Contact

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our DPO at privacy@alchemiststap.com.
