Our Origin

The Alchemist's Philosophy

Founded by master brewers dedicated to bringing commercial microbrewery quality into home kitchens.

Craft Without Compromise

We started The Alchemist's Tap because we were tired of stale grain sacks and inconsistent hop alpha acids. Every single malt lot we sell is floor-malted by historic European and PNW maltsters.

Nitrogen-Sealed Freshness

All hop lots are vacuum sealed with nitrogen flush within 24 hours of harvest.

Our Manifesto

"To elevate homebrewing from a kitchen hobby into an exact culinary art."
