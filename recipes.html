The Science of Mash Rest Temperatures

Beta-Amylase (140°F - 148°F)

Produces highly fermentable worts for dry, crisp body and higher ABV.

Alpha-Amylase (156°F - 162°F)

Produces unfermentable dextrins for rich body, sweetness, and head retention.

Water Profiles: Burton vs. Pilsen

Gypsum (calcium sulfate) accentuates hop bite in pale ales, while calcium chloride softens the palate for malty stouts and brown ales.

Quality Control

Troubleshooting Common Off-Flavors

Diacetyl (Butterscotch)

Cause: Yeast rushed. Fix: Allow 48-hour diacetyl rest before chilling.

Acetaldehyde (Green Apple)

Cause: Premature bottling. Fix: Give secondary fermentation an extra week.

DMS (Cooked Corn)

Cause: Weak boil with Pilsner malt. Fix: Boil uncovered for 90 mins.
